Prospect Scout

Find buyers already signaling intent on LinkedIn.

LinkedIn posts are real-time intent signals — but only if you can surface the right ones before they go cold. Prospect Scout scrapes posts by keyword, by URL, or by profile, scores every result against your ICP, and gives you an explanatory match narrative for each. No spray-and-pray. No manual filtering.

  • Three workflows: keyword search, post URL, or profile scan
  • ICP-aware scoring across industry, company size, seniority, and function
  • Per-post match narrative explains exactly why each prospect fits
  • Prospect Lists aggregate your best matches for outbound sequencing
  • Monitors run on schedule — Inbox shows new ICP-matched posts daily or weekly

AI Presence

See exactly where AI chatbots mention you — and where they don't.

Buyers are already asking ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Claude about the problems your product solves. Most early-stage teams have no idea whether they appear in those answers. AI Presence runs 20 queries across all four engines — awareness, consideration, category, and competitor-adjacent — and maps your visibility against theirs.

  • Structured 20-query scan across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Claude
  • Side-by-side competitor presence overlay — see who's winning which conversations
  • Prioritized gap list with specific content actions to close each gap
  • GEO score, share of voice, and platform-by-platform breakdown
  • Scheduled rescans (weekly / biweekly / monthly) to track momentum
